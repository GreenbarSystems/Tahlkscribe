What makes this hard to copy

Built local-first, all the way down.

Most ambient scribes are a thin AI layer bolted onto a cloud pipeline. Tahlk's architecture — and the compliance work underneath it — makes the easy version of this product a different, weaker product.

local / whisper.cpp

Transcription that never leaves the room

Speech recognition runs on your machine, on a bundled Whisper model — nothing to download, nothing uploaded. A competitor can't retrofit this after the fact; it has to be the foundation.

sign / sha-256

A note that can prove it wasn't altered

Every signed note is sealed into a running SHA-256 hash chain the instant you sign it. If the record is ever questioned, the chain — not your word — is the evidence.

scope / 42 cfr 2

Compliance sized for the hardest case

42 CFR Part 2, state behavioral-health confidentiality, the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule — the groundwork most scribes skip is already done, because behavioral health demanded it first.

os / content protect

Protections a browser tab can't make

Tahlk is a native desktop app, not a tab. That's what makes window content-protection possible — the session can't be screen-captured by something else running on the machine.

Managed by Greenbar: clinicians never hold an Anthropic key or manage a separate AI vendor relationship — every note-generation call runs through Greenbar's managed proxy, under Greenbar's BAA with Anthropic.

The loop

How a session becomes a signed note.

01

Record

Start recording at the beginning of the visit, stop at the end. Audio is saved locally and never uploaded.

02

Transcribe

Speech-to-text runs entirely on your computer on the bundled Whisper model. Nothing to install, nothing leaves the device.

03

Generate the note

Pick a template and generate a draft. This is the one deliberate step where the transcript is sent to Anthropic, covered by your BAA with Greenbar.

04

Review & edit

The draft is fully editable until you sign it — nothing is final until you say so.

05

Sign & export

Signing locks the note and seals a SHA-256 fingerprint into the chain. Export to clipboard or file whenever you're ready.
