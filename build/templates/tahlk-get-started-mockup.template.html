First-run setup

Five steps, once.

1

Your provider profile

Full name, credentials (MD, PMHNP-BC, LCSW, DPM, and so on), and specialty. Your name is what gets recorded as the signer on every note.

2

Set your Tahlk password

This password is local to this device — Greenbar never sees it, and can't reset it for you. That's why the next step exists.

3

Save your three recovery codes

Shown one at a time, with three ways to save each: copy to clipboard, save to your password manager, or download a printable PDF. We recommend at least two living in different physical locations.

4

Accept your BAA & EULA

A plain-language summary of the Greenbar relationship, with a checkbox that has to be checked before setup completes. This is what allows your first note generation to run without interruption.

5

Start using Tahlk

Setup is done. You land on the home screen, ready for your first session.

If you get stuck

Recovering access, and who to ask.

Forgot your password?

Enter any one of your three recovery codes to unlock, then set a new password. The two unused codes stay valid.

Lost your recovery codes too?

Contact your Greenbar representative before doing anything else — there may be options short of starting over.

Last resort: reinstall

Wipes every note, recording, and patient record on this device permanently. Only if password, all three recovery codes, and support are all exhausted.
